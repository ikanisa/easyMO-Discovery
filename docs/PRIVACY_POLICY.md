# Privacy Policy - easyMO Discovery

**Last Updated:** January 27, 2025

---

## 1. Introduction

easyMO Discovery ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our AI-powered mobility, marketplace, and payments platform.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide

- **Account Information:** User ID (anonymous if using guest mode), display name
- **Role Information:** Your roles (passenger, driver, vendor) - you can have multiple roles
- **Profile Information:** Phone number (optional), vehicle type (if driver/vendor)
- **Location Data:** Your location when using location-based features (only with your permission)
- **Conversation Data:** Messages sent to AI agents (stored for conversation continuity)
- **Business Listings:** If you create marketplace listings, we store listing details

### 2.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Location Data:** Only when you explicitly share your location for matching/search
- **Usage Data:** App usage patterns, features used
- **Device Information:** Browser type, operating system (collected automatically by hosting platform)

### 2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

- **Google Maps/Gemini:** When using location-based search (geocoding, business search)
- **Mobile Money APIs:** When generating payment QR codes (USSD format only)

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Core Functionality

- **Mobility Matching:** Match drivers with passengers based on location (location shared only during active search)
- **Marketplace:** Search and display business listings, enable contact between users and businesses
- **Payments:** Generate Mobile Money QR codes (USSD format)
- **AI Agents:** Provide AI-powered assistance (support, business search, legal help, real estate)

### 3.2 Data Storage

- **Conversations:** Stored in Supabase (encrypted, user-scoped)
- **Location Data:** Not stored beyond active session (used only for matching)
- **Presence Data:** Location published when online (driver mode), cleared when offline
- **Messages:** Stored for conversation continuity, user can delete conversations

---

## 4. Data Minimization

We follow data minimization principles:

- **Location:** Only collected when explicitly shared, not stored beyond active sessions
- **Phone Numbers:** Only collected if you choose to share (for business listings or contact)
- **Conversations:** Stored only for continuity, user can delete at any time
- **Roles:** Multi-role support (you control which roles are active)
- **No Tracking:** We do not use tracking cookies or analytics for advertising

---

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 With Other Users

- **Mobility Matching:** Location shared with matched drivers/passengers (only during active match)
- **Marketplace:** Business listings are public (active listings visible to all users)
- **Contact Information:** Phone numbers shared only when you choose to contact a match/listing

### 5.2 With Third-Party Services

- **Google Maps/Gemini:** Used for geocoding and business search (API calls, no data storage by Google)
- **Cloudflare:** Hosting and edge computing (standard platform data)
- **Supabase:** Database and authentication (encrypted, user-scoped)

### 5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety.

---

## 6. Data Security

- **Encryption:** All data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest (Supabase encryption)
- **Authentication:** Supabase Auth (industry-standard authentication)
- **API Keys:** Never exposed to client (all AI calls go through secure backend)
- **Row-Level Security:** Database policies ensure users can only access their own data

---

## 7. Your Rights

- **Access:** You can view your data through the app (profile, conversations, listings)
- **Deletion:** You can delete conversations, listings, and your account
- **Correction:** You can update your profile information
- **Location:** You can revoke location permission at any time (device settings)
- **Roles:** You can activate/deactivate roles at any time

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

---

## 9. Data Retention

- **Conversations:** Retained until you delete them or delete your account
- **Listings:** Retained until you remove them or delete your account
- **Presence Data:** Cleared when you go offline or close the app
- **Location Data:** Not stored beyond active session

---

## 10. International Data Transfers

- **Hosting:** Cloudflare (global CDN), Supabase (may store data in multiple regions)
- **Third-Party Services:** Google Maps/Gemini (may process data in multiple regions)
