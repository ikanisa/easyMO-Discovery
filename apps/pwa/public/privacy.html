Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 29, 2025

1. Introduction

easyMO Discovery ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our ChatGPT App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Location Data

We collect approximate location data (rounded to ~100m precision) to provide mobility matching and marketplace search services. Exact coordinates are never shared publicly or stored permanently.

2.2 User Account Data

We collect basic account information (user ID, role) to provide personalized services. Phone numbers are only shared with explicit user consent.

2.3 Usage Data

We collect anonymized usage data (tool calls, request IDs) for service improvement and debugging. This data is not linked to personal identifiers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide mobility matching services (driver/passenger matching)
  • Enable marketplace search and listings
  • Generate Mobile Money QR codes
  • Improve service quality and performance

4. Data Minimization

We practice data minimization:

  • Location data expires after 15 minutes (presence) or 10-15 minutes (ride intents)
  • Exact coordinates are never broadcast publicly
  • Location data is rounded to ~100m precision for display
  • Old data is automatically deleted (presence: 24 hours, intents: 7 days)

For more details, see our Data Minimization Statement.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We only share:

  • Approximate location (100m precision) with nearby users for matching
  • Contact information only with explicit user consent

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Row-Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Input validation on all API endpoints
  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Delete your account and data
  • Opt out of location sharing
  • Request data export

8. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, contact us at: privacy@easymo.discovery

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
