Data Minimization Statement

Last Updated: January 29, 2025

Our Commitment: easyMO Discovery collects and stores only the minimum data necessary to provide our services. We automatically delete data when it's no longer needed.

1. Location Data

Precision

  • Stored: Exact coordinates (for matching calculations)
  • Shared: Rounded to ~100m precision (0.001 degrees ≈ 111m)
  • Displayed: Area descriptions or approximate locations

Time-To-Live (TTL)

  • Presence Data: Expires after 15 minutes
  • Ride Intents: Expire after 10-15 minutes
  • Automatic Cleanup: Presence deleted after 24 hours, intents after 7 days

2. Update Throttling

To prevent data collection abuse:

  • Presence updates are throttled to minimum 10-second intervals
  • Faster updates are automatically ignored
  • Rate limiting: Max 5 ride intents per 10 minutes per user

3. Contact Information

  • Phone numbers are never shared without explicit user consent
  • Contact information is masked by default (shows last 4 digits only)
  • Full contact details require explicit "reveal contact" action

4. Automatic Deletion

Data Type TTL Cleanup
Presence (active) 15 minutes Marked offline
Presence (expired) 24 hours Deleted
Ride Intents (active) 10-15 minutes Marked cancelled
Ride Intents (cancelled) 7 days Deleted
Rate Limits 1 hour Deleted

5. No Persistent Tracking

  • We do not track users across sessions
  • No cookies or persistent identifiers
  • Request IDs are generated per-request and not stored long-term

6. Data Sharing

We share only:

  • Approximate location (100m precision) with nearby users for matching
  • Contact information only with explicit user consent
  • No exact coordinates are ever broadcast publicly

7. Compliance

Our data minimization practices align with:

  • GDPR principles (data minimization, purpose limitation)
  • Best practices for location-based services
  • ChatGPT Apps SDK privacy requirements
